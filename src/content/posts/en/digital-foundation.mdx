**Most business websites are digital brochures.**
They look nice. They list services. They have an "About Us" page.
But they don't **sell**.

## The Problem with "Just a Website"

A brochure website waits for people to call.
A **Sales System** guides them to buy.

## What Makes a Sales System?

1. **Clear Offer**: Within 3 seconds, the visitor knows what you sell and why they need it.
2. **Strong CTA**: "Book Now", "Buy Now", "Get Offer" buttons are prominent.
3. **Speed**: It loads instantly on mobile (where 90% of your traffic is).
4. **Trust**: Testimonials, case studies, and clear pricing build confidence.
5. **Tracking**: We know where every visitor came from and why they left.
