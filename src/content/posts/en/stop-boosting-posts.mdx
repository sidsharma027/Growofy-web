**Stop hitting that blue "Boost Post" button.**

It's the easiest way to give Mark Zuckerberg your money without getting any results.

## Why Boosting Fails

When you boost a post, Facebook optimizes for **engagement** (likes, comments, shares).
It does *not* optimize for **sales** or **leads**.

You get 500 likes. You feel good. But your bank account stays the same.

## The Real Way to Run Ads

Professional performance marketing (what we do at Growofy) involves:
1. **Ads Manager**: Using the backend tools, not the "Boost" button.
2. **Targeting**: Precision audiences based on behavior and interest.
3. **Retargeting**: Showing ads to people who visited your site but didn't buy.
4. **Tracking**: Knowing exactly how much revenue every ₹1 of ad spend generated.

**Vanity metrics (likes) don't pay the bills. Sales do.**
